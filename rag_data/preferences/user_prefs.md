# Personal Preferences - Harsh

## Location & Context
- Location: South Gujarat (Valsad region), India
- Timezone: IST (UTC+5:30)
- Climate: Hot & humid, monsoon June-September

## Energy & Focus Patterns

### High Energy Periods
- **Morning Peak**: 6:00-13:00 (best focus time)
- **Evening Recovery**: 17:00-20:00 (good for lighter work)

### Low Energy Periods
- **Afternoon Slump**: 13:00-16:00 (avoid important work)
- **Post-dinner**: 21:00+ (relaxation mode)

### Nap Schedule
- Time: 13:30-15:00
- Duration: 20-45 minutes
- Purpose: Recharge after lunch

## Daily Routines (Protected Time)

### Morning Temple Visit
- Time: 6:00-7:30
- Frequency: Daily
- Rule: No scheduling before 8:00 that conflicts

### Evening Tea
- Time: 17:00-18:00
- Purpose: Transition to evening work

### After-Dinner Walk
- Time: 21:00-22:00
- Duration: 20-40 minutes
- Frequency: Almost daily

### TMKOC Time
- Time: 13:00-14:15, 20:30-21:00
- Rule: No calls or work during this slot

## Scheduling Rules

### Deep Work
- **Best time**: 7:00-12:00 (morning)
- **Block size**: 90-120 minutes
- **Buffer**: 15-30 min before/after transitions

### Avoid Scheduling
- 13:00-16:00: Low energy, nap time
- 21:00-22:00: TMKOC/relaxation
- Before 8:00: Temple time

### Preferred Times
- Important tasks: 8:00-12:00
- Meetings: Morning or 17:30-20:00
- Light work: 17:30-20:00
